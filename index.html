About DVN

DVN is a campus-driven collective focused on UI/UX, Frontend, Content, and Integrations. We ship clean, responsive, and accessible web products with motion-first design.

What we do

Design systems, Figma prototypes, Next.js builds, form pipelines, and content ops.

How we do it

Small teams, strict sprints, ruthless QA, and user validation on every release.

Mission & Vision

  • Mission: Ship student-led products that feel premium and perform fast.
  • Vision: Become the most trusted campus product studio in India.
  • Values: Craft, speed, ownership, honesty.
